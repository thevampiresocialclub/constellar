# Constellations of the Gothic Sky

A naming set for an alien world whose people love spirituality and whose night sky teems with constellations. Names are built from fragments of botanical Latin, global art traditions (movements, techniques, artists), and beautiful or sacred things. Grouped by sonic role so the sky has texture — sharp stars, flowing rivers of stars, ceremonial wheels, ominous knots, tender clusters, and temple-wheels.

Pronunciation guides are included where helpful.
